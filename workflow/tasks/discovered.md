> **Re-deferral:** an open row that triage examines and deliberately parks is
> annotated in place — `open (re-deferred YYYY-MM-DD, reason)` — so triage can
> distinguish "looked at and parked" from "never looked at". Never a row move
> to deferred.md: cross-log moves break the bidirectional back-links.
> **Routing ledger:** a row terminates when the work is *routed* (promoted or
> rejected), never when it completes — there is no `completed` / `done directly`
> disposition; an ad-hoc in-session fix is recorded as retroactive promotion
> (create the task, mark it complete, point the row at it).
